SEO Questions

What businesses usually want to know first.

What is local SEO?

Local SEO improves how a business appears when people search for nearby services. It connects the website, Google Business Profile, local pages, reviews, citations, technical health, and content around the searches customers make in a specific market.

Do you only work with Berthoud businesses?

No. We are based in Berthoud and focus on Northern Colorado, including Loveland, Fort Collins, Longmont, Greeley, Erie, and surrounding communities. Technical and organic SEO work can also support businesses outside the immediate area.

How long does SEO take?

Technical fixes can improve crawlability quickly, but meaningful ranking and traffic growth normally develops over weeks and months as Google recrawls the site, content earns relevance, and local authority improves.

Do you guarantee first place on Google?

No. No legitimate SEO provider controls Google's rankings. We focus on measurable improvements to technical health, search visibility, content quality, local relevance, and lead opportunities.

Can you work on an existing website?

Yes. Most engagements begin with the existing website, Search Console data, Analytics, Google Business Profile, current rankings, and the pages already earning impressions.
